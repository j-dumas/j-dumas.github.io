Hi!

I'm **Jérémy**, a master's student in Computer Science. I study ICPS security using game theory to make decisions under uncertainty. 
For example, how to make the best actions possible for the security of a critical system attacked by an APT. 
I am also interested in systems, embedded programming, and of course cybersecurity.

I think security is more important than ever, and I also like solving challenges.
Unfortunatly, AI has taken most of the fun in finding vulnerabilities for the sake of speed.
In the real world, it makes to find and patch the fastest, but I also think one should be able to understand what was done, and for that, you need fundamentals.
I am currently trying to learn those.
I have participated in numerous CTFs over the years, mostly as a beginner, and am now trying to write my own writeups for those.

I'm a big Tolkien fan! I currently own most of his work in the original English.
