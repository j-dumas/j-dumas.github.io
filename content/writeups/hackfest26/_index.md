I had the chance to participate in the [Hackfest 2026 CTF](https://hackfest.ca/en/ctfs/hfctf/) in Quebec City this last 29-30 October.
Here are the writeups for the three flags I've found, and a bonus from an interesting IoT/SCADA flag I've missed.

HFCTF is a 24-hour competition between more than 60 teams of 6.
Teams take on dozens of challenges related, closely or loosely, to cybersecurity.
